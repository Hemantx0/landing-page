Nearby Folk Privacy Policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Nearby Folk ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use the Nearby Folk mobile application (the "App") and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

Nearby Folk is a nearby social discovery application made for users in India. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at support@nearbyfolk.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Profile Information

  • Email address, used to create and secure your account, sign you in, and authenticate your access through Firebase Authentication.
  • Display name, username, age, gender, bio, and interests.
  • Profile photo and any other media you choose to upload.

2.2 Location Information

Nearby Folk uses approximate device location to power nearby discovery features such as the Nearby radar and Recently Nearby. The Nearby radar supports real-time nearby discovery, while Recently Nearby may keep a temporary location and last-seen signal for up to 24 hours so people can discover recently active users within broad ranges up to 50 km.

We do not display your exact GPS coordinates or exact live location to other users. Other users may only see approximate distance ranges or buckets, not street, building, or map-level location.

We access your location only when you grant permission through your device settings. You can disable location access at any time from your device settings.

You may also enable Ghost Mode, where available, to stop appearing on supported nearby discovery surfaces.

2.3 Permissions

The App may request permissions such as location, camera, photos/media, microphone, and notifications depending on the features you use. You can manage these permissions from your device settings.

2.4 User-Generated Content and Visibility

We collect content you create or share through the Service, including chat messages, friend requests, voice room participation, community posts, Spaces or Stories, comments, reactions, and media you upload.

Some information you choose to share, such as your display name, profile photo, bio, interests, posts, comments, reactions, and participation in public or community features, may be visible to other users depending on the feature.

2.5 Device and Technical Data

  • Device model, operating system version, app version, language, and time zone.
  • App analytics events, such as screens viewed and features used.
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information.
  • Push notification tokens through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

2.6 Safety Data

We collect reports you submit, blocks you make, and moderation actions taken on your account so we can protect the Service and its users.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core features, including authentication, nearby discovery, chat, voice rooms, communities, Spaces, and friend requests.
  • To compute approximate proximity for the Nearby radar and Recently Nearby.
  • To send service-related and safety-related notifications.
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to abuse, harassment, security incidents, and platform misuse.
  • To improve the App through analytics, crash diagnostics, and product quality insights.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on service providers to operate the App, including:

  • Firebase by Google for authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, and Storage.
  • Google Play Services or Maps geocoding for location functionality on Android.
  • Other infrastructure or moderation providers needed to keep the platform reliable and safe.

Each provider has its own privacy practices, and your data is processed under their applicable terms.

Content safety checks with OpenAI

Where automated safety checks are enabled, we ask for your permission before sending submitted Spark images, captions and comments, and Space images and overlay text, to OpenAI to detect harmful content. Submissions may contain personal information you include in them. We do not add your account identifier, location or private chats to these screening requests.

We record your permission choice and safety-check results to operate moderation. OpenAI processes submitted content according to its API data policies. This is not a promise of zero retention. Declining or withdrawing permission prevents new content requiring these checks from being published; browsing remains available. Permission is remembered separately for Spark and Spaces. You can change either choice in Settings > Help > Spark safety checks or Space safety checks. Already-published content and existing safety records are not removed by withdrawing permission.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or show targeted advertising. We share data only:

  • With other users to the extent you choose, such as profile details, posts, or presence in voice rooms.
  • With service providers strictly to operate and protect the Service.
  • To comply with applicable law, a court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of the Service or its users.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data while your account is active. Temporary Recently Nearby location and last-seen signals expire after up to 24 hours unless refreshed by app activity. If you delete your account, your account enters a 45-day deletion grace window. During this period, your account is disabled and scheduled for deletion.

After 45 days, we delete or anonymize personal data except where retention is required by law or needed to enforce safety measures, such as records of abuse or bans.

7. Your Rights and Controls

  • Edit your profile, photos, and bio at any time.
  • Toggle location, push notifications, and Ghost Mode or Recently Nearby visibility controls, where available, from Settings.
  • Block or report any user, message, or post.
  • Request account deletion from in-app Settings. For the full process, see our Account Deletion page or email support@nearbyfolk.com.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), Firebase security rules, access controls, and monitoring or audit logs where appropriate.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children and Younger Users

Nearby Folk is open to users aged 16 and above. We rely on user-declared age at signup. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, and we do not show targeted advertising.

If you believe a user under 16 has registered, or if you are a parent who wants to remove a child's account, contact support@nearbyfolk.com. We will delete the account within 7 days. For users aged 16-17, all data is processed solely to provide the Service, with the same protections as adult users.

10. Distribution of the App

Nearby Folk is currently distributed through official app stores, including the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Users should only download or install Nearby Folk from official sources. We are not responsible for modified, repackaged, unofficial, or third-party versions of the App.

If you download Nearby Folk from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, your use of the App may also be subject to the applicable store’s terms, policies, and billing rules.

11. International Users

The Service is operated primarily for users in India. By using the Service, you consent to the processing of your information in India and other jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in-app or through the website. Continued use of the Service after such changes means you acknowledge the updated Privacy Policy.

13. Contact

Questions, concerns, or data deletion requests can be sent to support@nearbyfolk.com.
