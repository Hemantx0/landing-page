Nearby Folk Shipping and Exchange Policy

Digital Service

Nearby Folk is delivered as a mobile application and related digital service. We currently do not ship physical products, printed items, or merchandise through this website.

Shipping

Since all access is digital, no shipping fee, courier service, tracking number, or delivery timeline applies to standard Nearby Folk app access.

Exchange

Digital subscriptions and in-app purchases cannot be exchanged for another product. If there is a purchase or access issue, contact us and we will help you review the account or billing concern.

Contact

For shipping, exchange, or purchase-related questions, email support@nearbyfolk.com.
